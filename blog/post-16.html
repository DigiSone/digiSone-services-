How to Create a Sitemap and Submit It to Google (Step-by-Step)

Introduction: Why Google Can't Find Your Pages

You've published 50 blog posts. But Google only knows about 5 of them. Why? Because Google hasn't discovered the rest. A sitemap fixes this. It's a file that lists all your URLs, telling Google exactly what to crawl.

What is a Sitemap?

A sitemap is an XML file that lists all the important pages on your website. It helps search engines index your content faster.

Step-by-Step Execution

Step 1: Generate the Sitemap

Use a free tool like XML-Sitemaps.com. Enter your URL. It will crawl your site and generate a sitemap file.

Step 2: Upload the Sitemap

Upload the `sitemap.xml` file to the root of your website. It should be accessible at `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`.

Step 3: Submit to Google Search Console

Go to Google Search Console. Click "Sitemaps" on the left menu. Enter the URL of your sitemap and click Submit.

Common Mistakes

  • Submitting an outdated sitemap: Regenerate after adding new pages.
  • Blocking the sitemap in robots.txt: Ensure Google can access it.
  • Not verifying your site: You must verify ownership of your site in Search Console.

Practical Exercise

Generate and submit a sitemap for your website. Check back in a week to see how many pages Google has indexed.

Tools & Resources

  • XML-Sitemaps - Free generator.
  • Google Search Console - Submit here.

Conclusion

A sitemap is a simple but powerful SEO tool. Don't skip it.
