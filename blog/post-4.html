How to Deploy Your First Website Using Cloudflare Pages

Introduction: Why You Need to Master This

You've built your website. Now, how do you show it to the world? Traditionally, hosting was expensive and complicated. But today, we have Cloudflare Pages. It's free, ultra-fast, and connects directly to your GitHub. Let's get your site live in 5 minutes.

The Core Concept (Explained Simply)

Cloudflare Pages is a 'JAMstack' hosting platform. It takes your static files (HTML, CSS, JS) and serves them from the edge (CDN). This means your website loads incredibly fast anywhere in the world.

Real Code: Copy, Paste, and Run

# Terminal Commands (If using CLI) npm install -g wrangler wrangler pages project create my-site wrangler pages publish ./dist

Step-by-Step Execution

Follow these steps exactly:

  1. Step 1: Create a free GitHub account and upload your code.
  2. Step 2: Go to dash.cloudflare.com and sign up.
  3. Step 3: Navigate to 'Workers & Pages' -> 'Create Application' -> 'Pages'.
  4. Step 4: Click 'Connect to Git' and select your repository.
  5. Step 5: Leave the build settings blank (for static HTML) and click 'Save and Deploy'.
  6. Step 6: Your site is now live on a `.pages.dev` URL.
