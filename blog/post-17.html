UI/UX Design Masterclass: Principles Every Beginner Must Know

Introduction: Why Some Websites Just Feel Right

You've experienced it. A website that is a joy to use. Everything is where you expect it. The colors don't hurt your eyes. The text is readable. This is not an accident. It's the result of applying design principles. In this guide, I'll teach you the fundamentals.

The CRAP Method

  • Contrast: Make different elements stand out (e.g., black text on white).
  • Repetition: Repeat styles (colors, fonts) across the site for consistency.
  • Alignment: Align elements to create order. Avoid random placement.
  • Proximity: Group related items together.

Color Theory Basics

Use a color palette of 3-4 colors: a primary brand color, a secondary accent color, and neutral colors (black, white, gray) for text and background.

Typography

Use max 2 fonts: one for headings, one for body. Ensure readability with sufficient font size (16px minimum) and line height (1.5).

Step-by-Step Execution

  1. Pick a website you like.
  2. Analyze its colors and fonts.
  3. Try to recreate a single component (like a button) using Figma.

Common Mistakes

  • Too many fonts: Looks messy.
  • Low contrast: Makes text unreadable.
  • No whitespace: Cramming content makes it feel cluttered.

Practical Exercise

Redesign a poorly designed form using the CRAP principles. Submit your before/after to digisoneglobal@gmail.com.

Tools & Resources

  • Figma - Free design tool.
  • Coolors - Generate color palettes.
  • Google Fonts - Free fonts.

Conclusion

Good design is invisible. It just works. Practice these principles and your sites will look professional.
