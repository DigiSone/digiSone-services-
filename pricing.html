Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden fees?

No. All prices mentioned are transparent. Domain registration and hosting costs (if not included in the plan) will be clearly mentioned in your custom quote.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from Single Page to Starter, or from Starter to Business at any time. You only pay the difference.

What is the difference between Single Page and Starter?

A Single Page website is a one-page scrolling site (perfect for portfolios/landing pages). Starter includes up to 5 separate pages (Home, About, Services, Contact, etc.).

How do payments work?

We require 50% upfront to begin work and the remaining 50% upon completion. We accept UPI, Bank Transfer, and all major credit/debit cards.
