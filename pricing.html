Pricing FAQs

Are there any setup fees?

No. There are zero setup fees. You only pay the monthly plan cost. However, for custom domains, you may need to purchase the domain separately from a registrar like Namecheap.

Can I change my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. If you upgrade, the new price will be prorated for the current month.

Do you offer a refund?

Yes, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you are not satisfied with our service. Please refer to our Terms of Service for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.
