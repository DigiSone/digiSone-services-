Service FAQs

What is the difference between Single Page and Multi-Page Website?

A Single Page website has all content on one scrolling page (perfect for portfolios). A Multi-Page website has separate pages for Home, About, Services, Contact, etc. (better for SEO and larger businesses).

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes! Most clients combine Website Development + SEO + Logo Design. We offer bundle discounts for 2+ services.

How long does a project take?

Single Page: 3-5 days. Business Website: 1-2 weeks. E-commerce: 2-4 weeks. Custom Web App: 4-8 weeks. Timeline depends on complexity.

Do you provide content and images?

We provide premium stock images (Unsplash/Pexels). For content, we can write SEO-optimized copy for an additional fee, or you can provide your own.
