Our Development Process

A proven 6-step workflow that ensures transparency and quality.

1 Requirement Gathering

We understand your exact needs, user personas, and business logic. We document everything in a detailed scope document.

2 Wireframing & UI/UX

We create blueprints and premium designs for your approval. No code is written until you're 100% happy with the visual direction.

3 Architecture Planning

We design the database schema, API endpoints, and cloud infrastructure. This ensures the app is scalable from day one.

4 Agile Development

We code in sprints, providing you updates at every stage. Weekly demos keep you in the loop and allow for quick adjustments.

5 QA & Testing

Rigorous testing for bugs, speed, and security. We test on multiple devices and browsers to ensure perfection.

6 Launch & Support

We deploy to production and continue to monitor and maintain your product 24/7. Your success is our success.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

A simple 5-page website takes 2-3 weeks. A custom web app or e-commerce store can take 6-12 weeks depending on complexity. We provide a detailed timeline before starting.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. We follow a "Mobile-First" approach. Every project is tested on phones, tablets, and desktops to ensure a seamless experience across all devices.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes, we offer monthly maintenance plans that include security updates, backups, and performance monitoring. We don't disappear after launch.

Who owns the code and design?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, all intellectual property rights (code, designs, content) are transferred to you.
