Frequently Asked Questions

What is Edge Computing?

Edge computing means processing data closer to the user, rather than in a centralized data center. This reduces latency and improves speed significantly.

Will I lose data during migration?

No. We follow a strict migration protocol that includes backups and synchronization. We ensure zero data loss and zero downtime.

How much does cloud hosting cost?

It depends on your traffic and resource usage. Cloud is usually more cost-effective than traditional hosting because you only pay for what you use. We provide a free cost estimate.
